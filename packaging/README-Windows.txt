MSNX Delay - Windows
=====================

A recreation of the Akai MPC3000's built-in 3-channel delay.

INSTALL (about 1 minute)
------------------------
1. Copy the folder "MSNX Delay.vst3" into:

       C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3

   (Windows will ask for admin permission - click Continue.)

2. Open your DAW and rescan plugins. It shows up as "MSNX Delay".

That's it. Nothing else to install.

UPDATING FROM "MPC3K Delay"? This is the same plugin under a new name.
Delete the old "MPC3K Delay.vst3" folder from that VST3 folder first,
so your DAW doesn't show two copies. Saved projects keep working.

JUST WANT TO TRY IT?
--------------------
Double-click "MSNX Delay.exe" to run it on its own, no DAW needed.
In its Options/Settings, pick your audio interface and turn on an input.

IF WINDOWS SAYS "Windows protected your PC"
--------------------------------------------
This appears for apps from small developers. Click "More info", then "Run anyway".

QUICK START
-----------
- Channel 1 starts on. Set its Time to ~375 ms and Feedback to ~30%.
- Turn up Channel 2 and Channel 3 Volume for more echoes at other times.
- Careful: if the three Feedback knobs add up to more than 100%,
  the echoes grow forever (just like the real MPC3000). Keep your volume low.
